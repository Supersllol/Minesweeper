*AFFICHAGE INITIAL DE LA GRILLE
 
CH EQU $24
COUT EQU $FDED
TABV EQU $FB5B
HOME EQU $FC58
CROUT EQU $FD8E
SETINV EQU $FE80
SETNORM EQU $FE84
 
 ORG $8000
 
 JSR HOME
 LDA #16
 STA CH                  
 LDA #2                  
 JSR TABV                
 LDX #0                  
TITR LDA TITRE,X             
 CMP #$C1                
 BEQ OUT                 
 JSR COUT                
 INX                      
 BNE TITR                
 
OUT LDX #0                  
 LDA COL                 
 STA CH                  
 LDA LIGNE               
 JSR TABV                
 
CONTOUR LDA PRE,X               
 CPX #18                 
 BEQ NEXT                
 JSR COUT                
 INX                      
 BNE CONTOUR             
 
NEXT LDX #0                  
 LDA COL                 
 STA CH                 
 INC LIGNE               
 LDA LIGNE               
 JSR TABV                
 LDA LIGNE               
 CMP #19                 
 BEQ CONTOUR             
 CMP #20                 
 BEQ FINI                
 
 LDA #$FC
 JSR COUT
 
ECRIT LDA DATA                
 CPX #16                 
 BEQ EXTRA               
 JSR COUT                
 INX                      
 BNE ECRIT               
 
EXTRA LDA #$FC                
 JSR COUT                
 BEQ NEXT                
FINI RTS                      
 
COL HEX 0B                  
LIGNE HEX 04                  
PRE ASC "+----------------+"
DATA HEX FF                  
TITRE ASC "DEMINEURA"
